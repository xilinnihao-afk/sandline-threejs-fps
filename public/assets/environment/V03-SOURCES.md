# Sandline v0.3 environment additions

All building geometry, the gateway, signs, exposed-brick texture, balcony railings, roof terrace, laundry, cargo straps and ground patch layouts are original procedural work. No Counter-Strike / Valve models, textures or map geometry are included.

The existing v0.2 CC0 plaster, sandstone, paving and timber assets are retained.

## Dry earth surface

- Asset: **Brown Mud Dry**, Poly Haven.
- Page: https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_mud_dry
- License: CC0, https://polyhaven.com/license
- Source metadata: https://api.polyhaven.com/files/brown_mud_dry
- Retrieved: 2026-09-17.
- Original maps: 1k JPEG diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness.
- Files: `dirt-diff.jpg` (768 × 768), `dirt-nor_gl.jpg` (512 × 512), `dirt-rough.jpg` (512 × 512).
- The diffuse was desaturated, lightened and softened for the dusty map palette; all maps were downsampled and JPEG optimized for mobile loading.
- Source URLs:
  - https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/1k/brown_mud_dry/brown_mud_dry_diff_1k.jpg
  - https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/1k/brown_mud_dry/brown_mud_dry_nor_gl_1k.jpg
  - https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/Textures/jpg/1k/brown_mud_dry/brown_mud_dry_rough_1k.jpg
